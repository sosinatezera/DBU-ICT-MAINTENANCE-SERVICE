Features

Platform Capabilities

A comprehensive toolkit designed to streamline the entire ICT hardware and software maintenance lifecycle at Debre Berhan University.

Online Request Submission

Submit maintenance requests with device serial numbers, office location, issue description, and file attachments — no paper forms required.

Real-Time Ticket Tracking

Monitor your request through every stage — Pending, Assigned, In Progress, and Resolved — with live status updates.

Automated Technician Assignment

The system automatically routes requests to specialized technicians based on issue type — hardware or software — for faster resolution.

Role-Based Access Control

Three distinct roles — Requesters, Technicians, and Administrators — each with tailored dashboards and permissions secured via JWT.

Device Inventory & History

Track all computers, printers, and peripherals with asset tags, maintenance history, warranty status, and spare parts inventory.

Performance Analytics & Reports

Generate detailed reports on resolution times, technician performance, department trends, and issue categories with CSV export.

How It Works

Maintenance Workflow

Every request follows a structured, transparent lifecycle — ensuring accountability and timely resolution at each stage.

1
Submit Request
The user fills in a detailed form including device type, serial number, office location, issue category, and priority level.
Submitted
2
Ticket Generation
The system automatically generates a unique tracking code and creates a maintenance ticket in the queue.
Generated
3
Admin Review & Assignment
An administrator reviews the request and assigns it to a specialized technician based on issue type and workload.
Assigned
4
Physical Inspection & Repair
The technician inspects the device, performs diagnostics, and carries out the necessary hardware repair or software configuration.
In Progress
5
Resolution & Feedback
The user confirms the issue is resolved and provides a star rating to close the maintenance cycle.
Resolved

About Us

About the Platform

The Smart Computer Maintenance Service Request & Tracking System is designed to digitize and improve hardware and software maintenance request management for the community.

Our Mission

To provide a reliable and transparent digital platform for managing hardware and software maintenance requests, improving response time, accountability, and service delivery across the university community.

Our Vision

To create an efficient, transparent, and user-friendly digital maintenance workflow that simplifies ICT support and improves the experience of students, technicians, and administrators.

System Overview

A web-based maintenance management platform that replaces manual maintenance request processes with a centralized digital workflow for submitting, assigning, tracking, and managing ICT maintenance requests.
